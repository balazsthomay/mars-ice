LightGBM ensemble (5 seeds) trained on ~225,000 SWIM ice-consistency cells as soft labels, predicting from MOLA topography and Odyssey Neutron Spectrometer (epi/thermal/fast). Mars areoid dropped after a sanity check showed it carried partial latitude correlation. Validation against 1,356 globally distributed hard labels (Dundas crater scarps + Daubar new impacts + 7 in-situ landers) gives AUC 0.93, Brier 0.08; on the 996 tropical sites Daubar found dry, Brier 0.04 — the model correctly predicts low ice probability there even though those craters are not in training. Uncertainty = ensemble disagreement only — no proximity-to-label term, so "uncertain" means the model itself is unsure, not just that no rover went there.
